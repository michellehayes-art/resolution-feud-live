Privacy Policy for Resolution Feud

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Resolution Feud ("the Application"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our conflict-resolution game show app.

1. Information We Collect

Resolution Feud is designed for lighthearted couple and friend conflict resolution:

  • Gameplay Data: Team names, scores, and custom dispute scenarios you create are stored temporarily during your play session in your device's browser memory or local storage.
  • Optional Location: If you explicitly grant location access to celebrate your local city or match-up, your coordinates are processed strictly client-side on your device to determine your general locality. We do not track, store, or sell your precise location.
  • Audio & Buzzer Effects: Buzzer and prize announcement voices use standard browser synthesis or pre-recorded audio assets and do not record your microphone.

2. Third-Party Services & AI Processing

When you choose to generate custom dispute rounds using our AI question writer, the argument topic text you enter is securely processed by Google Gemini API in accordance with Google's API Privacy and Data Security standards to generate healthy resolution survey options. No personal identity records are stored or sold.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Game rounds and vouchers generated locally remain on your device. You can clear your game history or vouchers at any time simply by clearing your browser cache or app storage.

4. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, you can reach out via your Google Play account or app support.
